Station

The list shows stations described at the chosen data centre, nearest to the 2026 Rasuwa collapse first. Some do not share their recordings, or share them late; the test asks each one for its last 15 minutes.

Alarm rules

Notice: a new event appears in the lists. Warning: a long, slow signal reaches the station before any list entry, or a listed event does not look like an ordinary earthquake. Alarm: a listed event in glacier terrain shows at least two of these: shaking much longer than an earthquake record usually is at that distance, slow build-up, mostly slow shaking. The usual length is the time between the fastest and slowest earthquake waves over the distance (8 and 3 km/s) plus the ringing allowed above; the usual build-up is the time between the first wave and the strongest one (8 and 3.5 km/s). These are first rules written for this prototype. None of the limits has been calibrated with Himalayan records.

Connection log

Prototype for a class project. It is not a warning system and must not be used as one. Alarms from a past record are labelled REPLAY.

Event lists: EMSC SeismicPortal (CC BY 4.0) and USGS Earthquake Hazards Program. Waveforms: EarthScope and GEOFON (GFZ) data services and the operators of the station shown. Glaciers: Randolph Glacier Inventory 7.0, reduced to 0.05° cells. Borders: project data (COD-AB, Natural Earth). Waveform reading: seisplotjs 3.2.7 (MIT). Times are UTC; Nepal time is UTC + 5:45.
