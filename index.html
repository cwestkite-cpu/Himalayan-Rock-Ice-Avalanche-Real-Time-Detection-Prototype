Alarm rules

Notice: a new event appears in the lists. Warning: a long, very slow signal reaches the station before any list entry, or a listed event does not look like an ordinary earthquake. Alarm: a listed event in glacier terrain shows at least two of the signs below, or the shaking has not faded half an hour after it began.

Signs: the shaking builds up slowly; it is very slow (much of its energy below 0.2 Hz); it lasts much longer than usual for the magnitude and distance; it does not fade, or grows again. The limits were set on 11 Nov 2026 from the public record of station NK.KKN (Kakani, Nepal): the Rasuwa collapse of 26 Aug 2026 and six earthquakes of magnitude 4.3 to 5.6 at 200 to 440 km. Seven events are a small sample, and other stations may need other limits.

Connection log

Prototype for a class project. It is not a warning system and must not be used as one. Alarms from a past record are labelled REPLAY.

Event lists: EMSC SeismicPortal (CC BY 4.0) and USGS Earthquake Hazards Program. Waveforms: EarthScope and GEOFON (GFZ) data services and the operators of the station shown. Glaciers: Randolph Glacier Inventory 7.0, reduced to 0.05° cells. Borders: project data (COD-AB, Natural Earth). Waveform reading: seisplotjs 3.2.7 (MIT). Times are UTC; Nepal time is UTC + 5:45.
